Crop Profiles & Agronomy Database

Detailed cultivation guidelines, soil metrics, suitable varieties, and harvest indicators across core agricultural categories.

Paddy / Rice (Oryza sativa)

Major Cereal Staple

Thrives in warm, humid tropical and subtropical climates with abundant standing water or managed saturation.

  • Varieties: Basmati-370, IR-64, Swarna, Jasmine, Samba Mahsuri
  • Soil & pH: Clay or clayey-loam; pH 5.5 - 6.5 (low permeability)
  • Timeline: Kharif season (June - November); 110-150 days maturity
  • Harvest: When 80-85% grains turn golden yellow; moisture around 20%

Wheat (Triticum aestivum)

Cool Season Cereal

Essential rabi grain requiring cool days during vegetative growth and warm conditions during grain ripening.

  • Varieties: HD-2967, PBW-343, Sharbati, DBW-187, Kalyansona
  • Soil & pH: Well-drained fertile loam; pH 6.0 - 7.5
  • Timeline: Rabi season (November - April); 120-140 days maturity
  • Harvest: Grain moisture below 14% to ensure safe post-harvest storage

Maize / Corn (Zea mays)

High-Yield C4 Grain

Versatile cereal adaptable across wide temperature ranges, used widely for food, feed, and starch industries.

  • Varieties: Pioneer 3396, Ganga-5, Deccan-103, Sweet Corn Sugar-75
  • Soil & pH: Deep, well-aerated sandy loam to silt loam; pH 5.8 - 7.0
  • Timeline: Kharif or Spring; 90-110 days duration
  • Harvest: When husk leaves dry and black layer forms at grain kernel base

Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum)

Solanaceous Vegetable

High-value commercial horticultural crop demanding consistent sunlight and careful blight management.

  • Varieties: Arka Rakshak, Pusa Ruby, Roma, San Marzano
  • Soil & pH: Sandy loam rich in organic matter; pH 6.0 - 6.8
  • Timeline: Spring/Autumn; 70-90 days after transplanting
  • Harvest: Breaker stage for distant logistics; firm red for local markets

Potato (Solanum tuberosum)

Tuber Vegetable

Cool-weather root tuber crop requiring loose earthen ridges for optimal stolon expansion.

  • Varieties: Kufri Jyoti, Kufri Pukhraj, Russet Burbank, Kennebec
  • Soil & pH: Friable sandy loam; pH 5.2 - 6.4 (resists scab)
  • Timeline: Winter/Rabi; 80-120 days from seed tubers
  • Harvest: Dehaulming 10-14 days before harvest to firm tuber skins

Leafy Greens / Spinach (Spinacia oleracea)

Foliage Vegetable

Rapid-cycle nutritional green loaded with iron and provitamin A, suitable for open fields and hydroponic trays.

  • Varieties: Pusa Bharati, All Green, Bloomsdale, Savoy
  • Soil & pH: Nitrogen-rich loam with good drainage; pH 6.5 - 7.0
  • Timeline: Year-round in temperate bands; 30-45 days duration
  • Harvest: Cut-and-come-again harvesting early in cool mornings

Mango (Mangifera indica)

Perennial Tropical Fruit

King of tropical fruits, requiring distinct dry spell for profuse floral induction followed by warm summers.

  • Varieties: Alphonso, Dasheri, Amrapali, Himsagar, Kesar
  • Soil & pH: Deep alluvial loam with no hard pan; pH 5.5 - 7.5
  • Timeline: Flowering in Jan-Feb; harvest May-July
  • Harvest: Handpicked at physiological maturity with 1 cm pedicel stem

Citrus (Citrus reticulata / sinensis)

Subtropical Evergreen

Oranges, lemons, and mandarins with high market demand for fresh table juice and ascorbic acid content.

  • Varieties: Nagpur Mandarin, Valencia, Mosambi, Eureka Lemon
  • Soil & pH: Light, well-aerated sandy loam; pH 6.0 - 7.5 (salinity intolerant)
  • Timeline: 7-9 months from fruit set to harvest
  • Harvest: Clipped using blunt clippers when rind achieves color break

Banana (Musa acuminata)

Perennial Monocot Herb

Heavy water- and potassium-feeder providing rapid return on capital under regular drip fertigation.

  • Varieties: Grand Naine, Robusta, Dwarf Cavendish, Red Banana
  • Soil & pH: Rich loamy soil with continuous drainage; pH 6.0 - 7.5
  • Timeline: 11-13 months from sucker planting to harvest
  • Harvest: When top finger angles become round and ridge angles soften

Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum)

Major Fiber Crop

World's premiere natural textile fiber demanding high thermal units, warm weather, and frost-free periods.

  • Varieties: Bt Cotton hybrids (Bollgard II), Suvin, MCU-5
  • Soil & pH: Deep black cotton soils (vertisols) or alluvial loam; pH 6.5 - 8.0
  • Timeline: Kharif planting (May-June); 150-180 days maturity
  • Harvest: Multiple manual pickings as bolls burst open cleanly in dry sun

Jute (Corchorus olitorius / capsularis)

Golden Bast Fiber

Eco-friendly biodegradable packaging fiber grown in riverine delta basins with seasonal flooding.

  • Varieties: JRO-524 (Navin), JRO-204, JRC-321
  • Soil & pH: Deltaic alluvial soils and silt deposits; pH 6.0 - 7.5
  • Timeline: Pre-monsoon (March-May); harvested in 110-120 days
  • Harvest: Small pod stage followed by ribbon retting in slow-moving water

Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum)

Sugar & Biofuel Crop

Long-duration perennial grass producing sucrose, molasses, and ethanol with high biomass output.

  • Varieties: Co-0238, Co-86032, CoJ-64, CoLk-94184
  • Soil & pH: Deep, well-drained loams to clay loams; pH 6.5 - 7.5
  • Timeline: 10-14 months (autumn or spring planted setts)
  • Harvest: Cut close to ground level when brix refractometer measures 18-20%
Rice Agronomy Profile
Nutrition & Market Value:
78% complex carbohydrates, 7-8% plant protein. Global food security commodity with expanding markets for aromatic Basmati and red heirloom varieties.
Cultivation Protocols:

Puddled soil transplanting or Direct Seeded Rice (DSR). Seed rate: 20-25 kg/ha for line transplanting; 12-15 kg/ha for hybrid varieties.

Soil & Hydrology Requirements:

Impermeable subsoil clay with heavy water retention. Needs 1100-1500 mm water through crop cycle. Soil pH must be maintained between 5.5 and 6.5.

Harvesting & Post-Harvest Operations:

Harvest when panicles turn straw colored. Thresh and sun dry on clean tarpaulins until moisture reaches 12-14% to prevent Aspergillus mold contamination during storage.

Wheat Agronomy Profile
Nutrition & Uses:
High gluten protein (11-14%), essential dietary fiber, B-vitamins. Used in milling, pasta, bread-making, and commercial confectionery.
Cultivation & Seeding:

Seed rate: 100 kg/ha for normal sowing; 125 kg/ha for late broadcast. Depth: 4-5 cm. Optimum night temperature is 10-15°C for tillering.

Soil & Nutrient Demands:

Fertile, neutral loams (pH 6.0 - 7.5). Recommended standard N:P:K ratio is 120:60:40 kg/ha with half nitrogen split at CRI stage.

Harvesting:

Combine harvester or hand sickle when grain makes a crisp cracking sound between teeth. Clean and store in sealed metal bins.

Maize Profile
Agro-Ecological Specifications:

Optimal temperature: 21-27°C. Seed rate: 20 kg/ha for grain; 40 kg/ha for fodder. Sensitive to waterlogging; raised beds are strictly advised.

Market Value:

Crucial feedstock for poultry, animal feed, corn starch, ethanol, and high-fructose corn syrup production.

Tomato Cultivation Profile
Growth & Yield Highlights:

Requires staking with bamboo poles and trellising wires for indeterminate vines. High lycopene and vitamin C source. Yield reaches 50-80 tons/ha under greenhouse or mulched drip systems.

Potato Cultivation Profile
Tuberization Biology:

Optimal night temperature for tuber initiation is 16-20°C. Ridge earthing-up is performed 30 days after sowing to prevent solanine greening in exposed tubers.

Spinach & Leafy Greens Profile
Nutritional Profile & Fast Cycle:

Rich in iron, carotenoids, and folate. Requires short growing cycles (35-45 days), enabling up to 4 successional plantings per season.

Mango Orchard Profile
Orchard Maintenance:

Canopy pruning post-harvest encourages lateral productive flushes. Requires blossom protection against powdery mildew and mango hoppers during spring.

Citrus Cultivation Profile
Rootstock & Citrus Greening Defense:

Grafted onto disease-resistant rootstocks (Rough Lemon or Rangpur Lime). Routine psyllid surveillance is mandatory to stop Citrus Greening (Huanglongbing) vector transmission.

Banana Cultivation Profile
High Density Plantation:

Spacing of 1.8m x 1.8m produces over 3,000 plants per hectare. Continuous organic mulching around pseudostems retains essential root moisture.

Cotton Agronomy Profile
Fiber Technology & Boll Care:

Requires warm days and dry harvest windows. Pheromone traps monitor pink bollworm populations, preserving fiber lint quality.

Jute Fiber Profile
Fiber Retting Science:

Bundled stems are submerged in water for biological pectin dissolution (retting) over 12-15 days, resulting in lustrous golden bast fiber.

Sugarcane Profile
Sett Planting & Ratoon Crop:

Three-bud setts treated with fungicide are planted in furrows. Ratooning allows up to two subsequent annual harvests without replanting.
