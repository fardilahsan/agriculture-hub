Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

Ecological pest defenses, early scouting diagnostics, and non-destructive organic remedies.

Pillars of Integrated Pest Management

IPM balances cultural suppression, predatory biological insects, and biopesticides to control outbreaks without degrading soil microbiomes.

Preventative Measures

Cultural controls that disrupt pathogen cycles before establishment:

  • Crop Rotation: Alternate Solanaceous and Leguminous families to deplete pest reservoirs.
  • Sanitation: Remove infested stubble, crop residues, and fallen fruit harbors.
  • Trap Cropping: Plant border rows of marigold or mustard to attract insects away from core cash crops.
  • Soil Solarization: Cover damp summer soil with clear polyethylene for 4-6 weeks to sanitize fungal spores.

Biological Controls

Harnessing nature's food chain to preserve balance:

  • Predatory Insects: Release Ladybird beetles (Coccinellidae) and Green Lacewings against aphids and thrips.
  • Parasitoid Wasps: Deploy Trichogramma cards to parasitize eggs of stem borers and bollworms.
  • Companion Planting: Intercrop basil with tomatoes to repel hornworms and whiteflies.
  • Floral Corridors: Maintain flowering borders (alyssum, dill) to feed adult beneficial parasitoids.

Organic & Natural Remedies

Selective, non-residual botanical sprays:

  • Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%): Disrupts hormonal molting cycles of chewing larvae.
  • Potassium Horticultural Soaps: Dissolves soft waxy protective cuticles of mealybugs and mites.
  • Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt): Target-specific microbial endotoxin lethal only to lepidopteran caterpillars.
  • Garlic-Chilli Extracts: Natural volatile repellent suppressing sucking leafhoppers.

Monitoring & Early Detection Protocols

Weekly field scouting and anatomical inspections catch infestations below economic threshold levels.

Weekly Scout Recommended
Foliage & Leaves Symptoms
  • Curling & Honeydew: Sticky translucent secretions with black sooty mold indicate aphids or whiteflies.
  • Silvery Stippling: Bleached fine speckles on upper surfaces signal active two-spotted spider mite webbing.
  • Irregular Serpentine Trails: Winding white tunnels between leaf layers verify leafminer larvae activity.
Stems & Shoots Symptoms
  • Deadhearts / Wilting Central Shoots: Bored entry holes with frass pellets reveal stem borer or shoot fly damage.
  • Vascular Darkening: Longitudinal stem slicing displaying brown streaks points to Fusarium wilt colonization.
  • Stem Girdling: Bark nibbled right above soil collar points to nocturnal cutworm feeding.
Roots & Collar Symptoms
  • Root Knot Swellings: Clumpy gall nodules on root tap lines indicate Root-Knot Nematode (Meloidogyne spp.).
  • Root Shearing & Hollow Stubs: Plants easily pulled from dry soil signify white grub feeding.
  • Soft Collar Rot: Water-soaked dark lesions with white fungal mycelium signal Pythium damping off.

Active Infestation Response Guide

Safe, step-by-step containment protocol when pest densities exceed economic threshold levels without harming the wider soil biome.

1
Identification & Isolation

Capture specimens or high-resolution close-ups. Hand-prune severely struck branches and place inside sealed plastic disposal sacks.

2
Physical Disruption

Erect yellow sticky traps for whiteflies/aphids (12-15 per acre) and pheromone delta traps to catch male moths before reproduction.

3
Biopesticide Application

Apply cold-pressed neem oil (10,000 ppm at 3-5 ml/L) or targeted entomopathogenic fungi (Beauveria bassiana) during dusk to avoid photolysis.

4
Post-Treatment Audit

Re-inspect tagged plants 72 hours post-spray. If live nymphs drop below 80%, repeat foliar drench after 5 days with alternate bio-agent.
