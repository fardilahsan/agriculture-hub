Grains Monsoon / Kharif Conventional

System of Rice Intensification (SRI) Protocol

A water-saving method designed to increase lowland paddy productivity through young single-seedling transplanting and intermittent aeration.

Step-by-Step Procedure:
  1. Nursery Bed Prep: Raise nursery beds with rich organic humus; sow soaked seeds thinly.
  2. Young Transplanting: Transplant 8 to 12-day-old single seedlings at 25cm x 25cm spacing with roots intact.
  3. Water Management: Keep soil moist but not continuously submerged until flowering stage.
  4. Mechanical Weeding: Run rotary cono-weeders at 10-day intervals to aerate root zones.
Practical Tip: Avoid deep seedling burial; shallow horizontal transplanting speeds tiller initiation.
Grains Rabi/Winter Conventional

Wheat Sowing & Crown-Root Irrigation Cycle

Standard protocol for high-tiller winter wheat using zero-till seed drills and critical stage moisture scheduling.

Step-by-Step Procedure:
  1. Seed Treatment: Dress certified seeds with Trichoderma harzianum or Carboxin prior to sowing.
  2. Precision Seeding: Drill seed at 4-5 cm depth with 20 cm row spacing in November.
  3. Critical 1st Irrigation: Water accurately at Crown Root Initiation (21-25 days after sowing).
  4. Subsequent Irrigations: Provide irrigations at tillering, late jointing, flowering, and grain-filling stages.
Best Practice: Delaying the CRI irrigation by even one week can reduce tillers by up to 15%.
Vegetables Kharif/Summer Drip Irrigation

Precision Drip Irrigation for High-Yield Tomato

Optimized fertigation and moisture control for indeterminate tomato hybrids under silver-black plastic mulch.

Step-by-Step Procedure:
  1. Bed Construction: Form raised beds (90 cm width, 15 cm height) with 50 cm pathways.
  2. Drip Line Installation: Lay inline drip lateral (16mm, 40cm emitter spacing, 2 LPH discharge).
  3. Mulch & Transplant: Cover with 25-micron mulch, punch holes, and plant 25-day hardened seedlings.
  4. Fertigation Schedule: Inject water-soluble N-P-K (19:19:19) via venturi 2-3 times weekly.
Practical Tip: Keep soil moisture tension between 20-25 kPa during fruit setting to prevent blossom end rot.
Vegetables Rabi/Winter Organic

Organic Winter Spinach & Leafy Greens Production

Natural compost-driven soil feeding and neem-based pest deterrence for tender, pesticide-free foliage.

Step-by-Step Procedure:
  1. Soil Enrichment: Blend 10 tonnes/hectare well-decomposed vermicompost with neem cake.
  2. Broadcasting/Lines: Sow seeds in lines 15 cm apart and thin to 5 cm in cool weather.
  3. Foliar Feeding: Apply Jeevamrutha or fermented seaweed extract every 12 days.
  4. Sequential Harvest: Cut outer leaves 2 inches above ground level to allow multi-cut regrowth.
Best Practice: Harvest early in the morning when transpiration is low to maintain crisp leaf turgor.
Vegetables Kharif/Summer Hydroponic

NFT Hydroponic Leafy Green Setup

Recirculating Nutrient Film Technique (NFT) tailored for year-round lettuce and herb cultivation in controlled structures.

Step-by-Step Procedure:
  1. Channel Configuration: Set food-grade PVC/UPVC gullies on a 1:40 slope for continuous laminar nutrient flow.
  2. Seedling Plug Transfer: Germinate in rockwool or oasis cubes and transfer once roots protrude.
  3. Nutrient Solution: Maintain electrical conductivity (EC) between 1.4-1.8 mS/cm and pH between 5.8-6.2.
  4. Cycle Monitoring: Maintain 24/7 submerged pumps with backup aerator systems.
Practical Tip: Keep water temperature below 24°C; dissolved oxygen levels drop significantly in hotter solutions.
Fruits Monsoon Organic

Organic Banana Plantation & Sucker Management

High-density organic pit planting and desuckering regimen for robust Cavendish and Grand Naine stands.

Step-by-Step Procedure:
  1. Pit Preparation: Dig 60x60x60 cm pits; fill with farmyard manure, rock phosphate, and topsoil.
  2. Sucker Selection: Plant sword suckers (1.5-2 kg) with conical base and narrow leaves.
  3. Desuckering: Remove side suckers regularly until fruit initiation to avoid nutrient drainage.
  4. Bunch Protection: Bag immature bunches with perforated blue polypropylene sleeves against insect scarring.
Best Practice: Retain only one follower sucker when the main plant reaches 75% flowering stage.
